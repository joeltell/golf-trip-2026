Welcome

Ten rounds in six days, three of us, and a collective handicap nobody has volunteered to verify. The idea is to take on every course in front of us with the clubs we own and whatever swing turns up that morning.

There will be hope on the first tee and arithmetic on the last. There will be one drive so pure we talk about it for the rest of the week, and a great many Jerrys. Mostly there will be three grown men laughing in a field, which was always the point.

August 17–22, 2026 — Jönköping down to Ulricehamn, up through Skövde and Lidköping, home via Åmål. Schedule, courses, players and the leaderboard are all up in the menu.
